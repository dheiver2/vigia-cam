VMS · Video Management System

Intelligent video monitoring
with 100% on-device AI

Native desktop VMS for macOS that monitors multiple RTSP/HLS cameras on a live videowall, with real-time person and vehicle detection (YOLOv8n on Neural Engine), smart rule-based alarms, license plate reading (LPR), camera map, and zone analytics (intrusion, loitering, absence). Evidence with SHA-256 chain of custody, searchable SQLite event history, role-based access, built-in auto-update — built for CCTV and public security deployments.
